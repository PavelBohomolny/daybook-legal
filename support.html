Help & Support

Contact email with your Daybook version, iOS version, device model and a description of the issue. Include a redacted screenshot if useful.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or App Check debug tokens. Avoid attaching private journal content unless you specifically want support to see it.

My Content Is Missing

Confirm you used the same Daybook account and sign-in method as before. Apple and Google sign-ins can represent separate accounts. Check your internet connection and the app's sync status. Do not delete the app while investigating unsynced content.

A Photo Will Not Save

Check your connection, device storage, photo permissions and any error shown in Daybook. Wait for a save to finish before leaving the editor. If it fails, keep the original photo and contact support with the error and app version.

Manage or Restore Daybook Pro

Open your profile and subscription screen to restore purchases or manage the subscription. You can also use Apple's subscription settings. Confirm the Apple Account used for the purchase is the one currently used for App Store purchases. If restoration fails, contact support; never send full payment details.

Cancel or Request a Refund

Cancel in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting Daybook or your account does not cancel billing. Apple handles refund requests through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete an Account or Request Your Data

Open Daybook's profile, then its account screen and choose Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps. If you cannot access the app, or need help with a data access or deletion request, email. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you.

Change Privacy Settings

Open Profile, Settings, then Privacy & Face ID. The usage-sharing control changes optional usage and crash reporting. Manage calendar, microphone, Photos and notification permissions separately in iOS Settings.
