Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Draft for review. Developer identity, processing grounds, diagnostics retention and consent settings must be finalized before release.

Daybook ("we", "our", "us") helps you manage your calendar, tasks, notes, moods and photos. This policy describes how information is processed when you use the app. For privacy requests, contact bohomolnyi.dev@gmail.com.

Information We Process

  • Account information: account identifiers, sign-in provider, and the name or email shared by Apple or Google. Apple may provide a private relay email address.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add, such as a name, preferred name, birthday, pronouns, profile photo, goals and life areas.
  • Your content: tasks, plans, events, journal notes, mood entries, photos, captions and audio attachments you choose to create or upload.
  • Subscription information: purchase identifiers, subscription status, expiration or renewal information, and a Daybook account identifier used to associate access with your account.
  • Usage and diagnostics: when enabled, feature-use events, app and operating-system versions, device information, installation identifiers and crash diagnostics. Our custom usage events describe actions such as completing onboarding, creating a task or adding a photo; they do not include the text or media you enter.
  • Security and support information: app-attestation signals, technical request metadata and information you provide when contacting support.

Why We Process Information

We process account information and content to provide the features you request, synchronize signed-in content, manage subscription access and respond to support requests. We use security information to protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access. Optional usage reporting and crash diagnostics help us understand feature use and investigate faults.

Where European data-protection law applies, providing the requested service may rely on performance of a contract, protecting the service may rely on legitimate interests, and retaining records may be necessary to meet legal obligations. Where consent is required for optional diagnostics or particular information, that consent must be obtained and can be withdrawn. A settings switch alone does not replace any legally required consent.

Device Storage and Cloud Sync

Daybook keeps local data and cached media on your device. When you use a signed-in account, supported account content is also stored using Firebase cloud services so it can synchronize across your devices. Photos and audio attachments are uploaded to cloud storage; photos may be resized and compressed before upload. Daybook is not a device-only or iCloud-only service.

Calendar, Photos, microphone and notification access depend on the features you choose and the permissions you grant. Connected device calendars are accessed through Apple's calendar services. Calendar providers you already use may process those events under their own policies. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Service Providers and Sharing

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, Analytics and Crashlytics support accounts, synchronization, storage, security and diagnostics. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Apple and Google: provide the sign-in methods you choose. Apple also processes App Store billing. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: processes app-user identifiers, transaction information, subscription access and technical information to manage Daybook Pro. We do not intentionally send your journal text, mood entries, photos or audio to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these public legal and support pages and may process connection information such as IP addresses. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising scripts or contact forms. See GitHub's privacy statement.

We do not sell personal information or use an advertising SDK in Daybook. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

Subscriptions and Payments

Apple handles App Store payments. We do not receive your full payment-card details. RevenueCat associates subscription access with your Daybook account. Signing out, uninstalling Daybook or deleting your Daybook account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it through your Apple Account.

Security and International Processing

Daybook uses authenticated access, access-control rules and provider-managed encryption in transit and at rest for cloud services. These protections are not end-to-end encryption: data is processed by us and our service providers to operate the app. Face ID or an app lock controls access on your device; it does not separately encrypt your cloud content.

Service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their applicable data-processing agreements and transfer safeguards must cover those transfers. Contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your data. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

Account content is retained to provide your account until you delete it or request deletion, subject to necessary legal retention. You can delete your account from within Daybook. The deletion process removes associated account content from the active Firebase database and storage and deletes the Firebase authentication account. Temporary deletion markers prevent stale sessions from recreating data; failed cleanup can require a retry.

Data you exported, copies on offline devices, provider backups and records subject to legal obligations may persist separately. Usage and crash data follow the configured provider retention settings. Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are separate from your Firebase account; deleting the account does not automatically erase all of those records. Contact us for related access or deletion requests. We do not promise immediate deletion of every provider backup or transaction record.

Your Choices and Rights

You can edit your profile and content, manage device permissions, and switch usage reporting and crash reporting off in Daybook's privacy settings. These reports currently default to enabled; disabling them does not retrospectively delete previously submitted information.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and withdraw consent where it is used. Contact bohomolnyi.dev@gmail.com; we may need to verify your identity. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Providing account information is necessary for signed-in synchronization; optional profile fields are not required.

Children and Sensitive Content

Daybook is a general journal and planner, not a medical service or a service designed specifically for children. Notes and mood entries may reveal sensitive information. Consider what you choose to store and synchronize. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information without any authorization required by applicable law.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy as the service changes and will revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated where required. Questions and privacy requests: bohomolnyi.dev@gmail.com.
